This time of the year, for the last decade, I've been celebrating
being a Research Software Engineer (RSE) with more and more people
every year. [Society of Research Software Engineering][socRSE]'s
[RSECon][rsecon] turns 10 this year and I've participated in most of
them. Making a lot of friends on the way.

This year the celebration it is (was for me!) going to be even larger
as [Research Software Allieance][resa]'s [International Research
Software Conference][irsc] is co-located and happening just before
RSECon. Two conferences celebrating Research Software in one week. It
sounds like a dream.

And for me, it will be still a dream...

Both conferences are happening at [the University of
Sheffield][uni-sheffield], a place I keep in my heart as it was were I
taught my first [SunPy][sunpy] and [Astropy][astropy]
[workshop][oa-workshop] and where many of the Sunpy early developers
were based. However, the management at [Sheffield][uni-sheffield] has
been playing dirty games. After [staff went on 16 days of strike last
year][strike-sheffield], and therefore classes were missed. The
management of the university decided ["double deducting" to those who
refused to deliver these missed classes][double-deducting], for which
they haven't already been paid.

In the light of these circumstances, [University and College Union
(UCU)][ucu] has [called their members to boycott any activity involved
with the university][ucu-boycott], such as participating on
conferences being held there.


In short, I'm not celebrating in #Sheffield this week.


It's hard because I know [Sheffield][uni-sheffield] wont even notice
that I won't be there. And I know that [RSECon][rsecon-withdraw] and
[IRSC have both evaluated the financial impact for our
communities][withdraw] withdrawing and therefore loosing a large
deposit. But, though I understand that our financial impact to the
university is small for them but large to us, I also believe our
personal and academic community impact is more important. This is why
I withdrew the sessions I was taken part on.  By writing here I wish
my words reach to other RSEs and maybe Sheffield's future students and
staff, so they know what type of values are followed by their
management.

To all staff affected: ✊ Solidarity!
